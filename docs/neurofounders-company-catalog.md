## Evidence and map treatment

NeuroFounders is used as a discovery and classification source, not as proof of efficacy. New records include the official website supplied by each NeuroFounders profile plus the profile itself. They receive an E1 evidence level until product-specific primary trials, papers, or regulatory pages support a stronger claim.

The source profiles expose country rather than city-level headquarters. New pins therefore use an explicitly labeled **Country-level location** with representative country coordinates; the map does not present those points as verified headquarters.
