# European organization evidence audit

Audited on 2026-08-03. The scope contains every catalog organization headquartered in a conventional European state, plus transcontinental Russia, Turkey, and Kazakhstan and the South Caucasus. This intentionally errs toward inclusion.

## Result

- Organizations audited: 352 (247 companies and 105 academic programs).
- Organizations with canonical activity before this pass: 52.
- Added papers: 125. Every one of the 105 academic programs has a canonical paper after combining prior and new data.
- Added sponsor/collaborator-matched or explicitly named program/device trials: 64.
- Added trial milestones and upcoming registry checkpoints: 40.
- Added limited program profiles where no stronger canonical activity qualified: 152.
- Organizations with canonical activity after this pass: 352 of 352.
- Existing profile research links cover 158 organizations with papers and 77 with video/channel resources; 23 links resolve to specific YouTube videos rather than generic channels.

## Evidence rules

- DOI, PubMed, publisher metadata, OpenAlex institution identifiers, and sponsor/collaborator-matched ClinicalTrials.gov records are used as structured evidence leads. Two registry records were included through a separately checked named-program or named-device relationship, with the academic sponsor left explicit.
- A paper affiliation proves that the organization participated in that publication; it does not validate every program at the institution.
- A registered trial proves a public protocol and status, not safety, efficacy, or a positive outcome. Estimated completion dates are watch points, not promised readouts.
- YouTube channels remain profile research resources. A video is not promoted to a dated canonical demo unless its date and content are verified.
- Organizations without a qualifying paper, trial, milestone, or dated video receive a limited project record tied to an official/profile source; those records explicitly do not claim demonstrated performance.

- If a prior catalog lead cannot be corroborated as currently operating, it remains visibly marked E0 rather than being presented as an active program; this applies to Implex in this snapshot.
