# U.S. organization evidence audit

Audited on 2026-08-03. The scope contains every catalog organization headquartered in the United States.

## Result

- Organizations audited: 215 (136 companies and 79 academic programs).
- Organizations with canonical activity before this pass: 73.
- Added papers: 85. Every one of the 79 academic programs has a canonical paper after combining prior and new data.
- Added exact-sponsor trials: 54.
- Added trial milestones and upcoming registry checkpoints: 30.
- Added limited program profiles where no stronger canonical activity qualified: 62.
- Organizations with canonical activity after this pass: 215 of 215.
- Existing profile research links cover 88 organizations with papers and 34 with video/channel resources; 36 links resolve to specific YouTube videos rather than generic channels.

## Evidence rules

- DOI, PubMed affiliation searches, publisher metadata, and exact-sponsor ClinicalTrials.gov records are used as structured evidence leads.
- A paper affiliation proves that the organization participated in that publication; it does not validate every program at the institution.
- A registered trial proves a public protocol and status, not safety, efficacy, or a positive outcome. Estimated completion dates are watch points, not promised readouts.
- YouTube channels remain profile research resources. A video is not promoted to a dated canonical demo unless its date and content are verified.
- Organizations without a qualifying paper, trial, milestone, or dated video receive a limited project record tied to an official/profile source; those records explicitly do not claim demonstrated performance.
