# Russia and Asia Coverage Audit

Audit date: 2026-07-31

## Scope and standard

This pass checked Russia, Western Asia, Central Asia, and East Asia outside China. It used broad web discovery but promoted only organizations with a direct institutional, company, government, or paper source that establishes actual BCI, neural-interface, neurostimulation, or BCI-enabling work. A single news mention, investor relationship, course title, or generic neuroscience program was not enough by itself.

The tracker is intentionally broader than implanted communication BCI, but every adjacent record says what it actually is. EEG acquisition companies, educational kits, DBS programs, and announced centers are not presented as equivalent to human BCI restoration.

Private-company "net worth" was not estimated from revenue, fundraising, or product prices. A valuation is shown only when a source states one; otherwise the profile says it is not publicly disclosed.

The audit did not find a durable source-clear BCI organization in Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, or Turkmenistan. This is not evidence that no researchers or projects exist there; it means none cleared the stated inclusion threshold in this pass.

### East Asia outside China

Japan, South Korea, and Taiwan were not empty: they already had 24 catalog records. The audit therefore focused on gaps and duplicates rather than adding more generic university profiles. One Mongolian project cleared the minimum source threshold:

- Mongolian University of Science and Technology BCI Research, Ulaanbaatar: an institution-hosted 2023 proceedings paper reports an exploratory EEG system that classified imagined light-control commands at 79.5%. The profile is E1 and explicitly says this is one documented project, not a mature lab or clinical system.

No credible open source for an active North Korean BCI organization was found. Existing Japanese, South Korean, and Taiwanese records were retained without duplicate additions.
