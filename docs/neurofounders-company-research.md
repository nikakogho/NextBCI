## Evidence policy

- NeuroFounders is a structured discovery source, not proof of clinical benefit.
- Founder and city notes are only promoted when the official-site pass produced a reasonably attributable lead. Otherwise the field says not verified.
- Private-company “net worth” is not guessed. The company-value field says not publicly disclosed unless a reliable valuation was found; public-company values are treated as dynamic.
- Official-company milestones are labelled company-reported. Papers and videos are linked as research resources, not automatically promoted to NextBCI evidence milestones.
- All profiles were researched on the same date so later refreshes can be audited.
